Dataset and model

CityEye's live cleanliness reading comes from a model trained on a public Kaggle dataset of clean and littered roads. This page shows the data, how well the model scores, and lets you test it in your own browser.

Dataset

Clean/Dirty Road Classification, from Kaggle: kaggle.com/datasets/faizalkarim/cleandirty-road-classification. Photos of roads labelled clean or dirty (littered). Duplicates were removed and the images were split 70 / 15 / 15 into training, validation and test sets, keeping the same clean-to-dirty ratio in each.

Sample images

How the model was trained

  1. Every image is resized to 224 × 224 pixels.
  2. MobileNetV2, a network pre-trained on ImageNet, turns each image into 1,280 numbers that describe what it shows.
  3. A logistic regression classifier learns from those numbers to tell clean roads from littered ones. Its setting is chosen with 5-fold cross-validation, and mirror images are added as extra training data.
  4. The classifier is tested on images it never saw during training.
  5. The result is a small file (about 20 KB), model/cleanliness_model.json, that the app runs live on the phone.
